# Glossary

[← README](../README.md)

The model's own terms. Each rule is stated only in its own document; this page says what a word
means and where to read more.

| Term | Means | See |
|---|---|---|
| **Gross profit** | net sales without VAT less the cost of sales | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#gross-profit-and-gross-margin) |
| **Gross margin** | gross profit as a share of net sales without VAT | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#gross-profit-and-gross-margin) |
| **Plan margin** | the margin at the regular price, before any discount | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#the-plan-margin-at-the-regular-price) |
| **List price** | the regular selling price, before any discount | [business.md](business.md#prices) |
| **Net price** | the selling price after both discounts, with VAT | [measures.md](measures.md#two-sales-bases) |
| **ex VAT** | a sales figure with the VAT of each line taken out | [measures.md](measures.md#two-sales-bases) |
| **Extra discount** | the discount after the regular one, on the invoice line | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#the-extra-discount-against-the-marketing-budget) |
| **Index** | the change against last year: (this year − last year) ÷ last year | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#year-on-year) |
| **Outlets** | outlets with a sale in the window (numeric distribution) | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#reach--sales-per-outlet) |
| **Sales per outlet** | net sales over the outlets with a sale | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#reach--sales-per-outlet) |
| **Distribution effect** | the part of the sales change that came from more or fewer outlets | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#reach--sales-per-outlet) |
| **Sales-per-outlet effect** | the part that came from each outlet buying more or less | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#reach--sales-per-outlet) |
| **Volume effect** | the part of the sales change that came from selling more or fewer units | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#price--volume) |
| **Price effect** | the part that came from a higher or lower price | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#price--volume) |
| **Verdict** | the pattern behind a change, named by the signs of its two effects | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#price--volume) |
| **Kept / new / lost** | products or outlets with a sale in both windows, only now, only a year earlier | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#kept-new-and-lost) |
| **Trade budget** | what the price sets aside for marketing against what is spent | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#the-trade-budget) |
| **Accumulated** | what the price sets aside, and what suppliers give back | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#the-trade-budget) |
| **Spent** | trade terms, extra discounts and credit notes issued | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#the-trade-budget) |
| **Trade term** | a bonus, placement, campaign or promotion agreed with a customer for an outlet and a brand | [gross-margin.md](gross-margin.md#the-trade-budget) |
| **Outlet** | a customer's point of sale | [business.md](business.md#customers-outlets-and-channels) |
| **Actual Year** | this year to date, cut at today | [measures.md](measures.md#time-windows-are-functions) |
| **Prior Year YTD** | last year, cut at today's date one year back | [measures.md](measures.md#time-windows-are-functions) |
| **Organisational unit** | a warehouse or van documents are booked in | [model.md](model.md#dimensions) |
